Two adjustments so we stay true to Tim's principles:
- **Fix PRs stay clean.** A PR that fixes a real bug, with the mainnet transaction that proves it, and no plug for
  Pulse inside it. A maintainer who wants to know where it came from sees the author's profile and the README. If we
  add `npx` lines to strangers' PRs we're back to the promo footers you warned about.
- **3-5 PRs a day is too many for review quality.** I'd start at 1-2 a day, each one checked on mainnet, and only to
  repos with recent commits, so they get merged instead of ignored.
